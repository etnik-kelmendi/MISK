# Decisions

Each entry: what was decided, why, and when. Newest last.

| ID | Date | Decision | Why |
| -- | ---- | -------- | --- |
| D1 | 2026-11-04 | MISK is its own repository, not part of `shb-solutions`. | SHB forbids any price in its code by an automated check; MISK is built around prices. Two products, two histories. |
| D2 | 2026-11-04 | Same stack as SHB: Node 24, Fastify, PostgreSQL with `node-pg-migrate`, React 19 + Vite, Vitest, Playwright. | Proven in the sibling project; one set of tools to run and maintain. |
| D3 | 2026-11-04 | Phase 1 first: one-off bookings, payments, pro onboarding, dispatch, admin. Monthly plans come after the Phase 1 gate. | The demo's own roadmap; gives a usable product soonest. |
| D4 | 2026-11-04 | Phase 1 ships as one responsive web app with three areas (`/`, `/pro`, `/admin`). Native apps are Phase 3. | The demo's roadmap puts native apps in Phase 3; the web app reuses the same API. |
| D5 | 2026-11-04 | Stripe Connect: card held with a manual-capture PaymentIntent, captured on completion, pro paid by a separate transfer to their Express account, weekly Friday payouts. | The pro is chosen only after booking, so a destination charge at booking time is impossible. Separate charges and transfers fit dispatch. |
| D6 | 2026-11-04 | Bookings can be made at most 6 days ahead. | Card holds expire after 7 days; the job must finish while the hold is valid. |
| D7 | 2026-11-04 | Coverage uses served postcodes with centre coordinates managed by admins; a pro covers a postcode when it is within their travel distance of their home postcode. | Keeps the demo's travel-distance slider without a paid geocoding service. |
| D8 | 2026-11-04 | Prices are integer euro cents everywhere. Size multipliers are stored per service as percentages (default S 100, M 130, L 170, from the demo). | No floating-point money. |
| D9 | 2026-11-04 | Customers and pros sign in with a 6-digit SMS code; admins with username and password. Apple and Google sign-in wait for accounts (see `QUESTIONS.md` Q1). | Phone sign-in needs only Twilio, which the team already uses. |
| D10 | 2026-11-04 | Without Twilio settings, sign-in codes are written to the API log, and only when `NODE_ENV` is not `production`; production refuses to start without Twilio. | Local development works offline; production can never silently drop codes. |
| D11 | 2026-11-04 | Production refuses to start without Stripe settings. Tests and the end-to-end server inject a fake payment gateway that lives under `test/`. | No booking can ever be taken without real payment in production. |
| D13 | 2026-11-04 | A slot is offered when free eligible pros outnumber the same trade's bookings at that time that are still waiting for a pro. Bookings in one trade are taken one at a time (advisory lock), and a database constraint stops any pro holding two overlapping jobs. | Never promises a pro who is not there. Counting demand per trade, not per postcode, may hide a slot at busy times; it never overbooks. |
| D14 | 2026-11-04 | Dispatch order: highest average rating (4.5 assumed for a new pro), then fewest jobs in the last 7 days, then shortest distance. One offer at a time, 15 minutes each. | The demo: "offered to the best-rated free pro nearby"; spreading work keeps new pros engaged. |
| D15 | 2026-11-04 | Completion needs at least one photo. Unanswered extra work is dropped at completion. Capture and payout run right after completion and are retried every minute until done. | The demo's "add photos & complete"; nothing is charged without approval; a provider outage never loses money. |
| D12 | 2026-11-04 | Uploads (documents, photos) are stored on local disk under `UPLOAD_DIR` with random names, checked by content signature (JPEG, PNG, PDF), at most 8 MB each, and served only through access-checked routes. | Simple to run for a one-city pilot; storage sits behind one module so it can move to object storage. |
| D16 | 2026-11-04 | Admins can recover a forgotten password by email: a 30-minute, single-use link to the recovery email on their account, sent through any SMTP service. The link's token sits after `#` so it never reaches server logs; only its hash is stored; the answer never reveals whether an account exists; requests are rate limited. Customers and pros have no password. | Requested by the owner. Admins without an email still have the Team page and the server command. |
| D17 | 2026-11-04 | Customers and pros can also register with first name, last name, email and password (8+ characters, a letter and a number), accepting the terms; the time of acceptance is stored. Email and password log-in sits next to phone codes on the log-in page; new accounts then choose customer or pro. Messages use correct spelling ("email address", "Passwords do not match"). | User story from the owner. Phone sign-in stays, because pros need a phone for job-offer texts. |
| D18 | 2026-11-04 | Wrong passwords never lock an account or make anyone wait 15 minutes; each failed password log-in (customers, pros and admins) answers about 1 second later instead. Phone sign-in codes keep their limit of 5 guesses per code. | Owner's request: people may retry as often as they need. The delay keeps automated password guessing slow; a 6-digit code would be guessable without its limit. |
| D19 | 2026-11-05 | Plan "Visit length" is the length of each visit in minutes (15–600, in steps of 15), separate from "Minimum term" in months. The optional extra needs a monthly price once it has a name. The plan page lists what is still missing instead of only disabling Save and Publish. | Confirmed by the owner; the story's visit-length text repeated the minimum-term text. |
| D20 | 2026-11-05 | The admin control panel is its own website (`ADMIN_ORIGIN`, built with `vite --mode admin` into `dist-admin`); the customer and pro app has no admin pages. Each site has its own session cookie, admins sign in only on the control panel and customers and pros only in the app, and reset links point to the control panel. Both use the same API and database. | Owner's request: the app and the control panel must not be connected. Without `ADMIN_ORIGIN`, the API still serves both from one origin. |
| D21 | 2026-11-05 | New team members can request an admin account on the control panel ("Request an account" on the sign-in page). The account cannot sign in or get a reset link until an existing admin approves it under Settings → Team, where requests can also be declined. Approved admins are emailed about each request. Admin passwords keep their 12-character minimum. | Owner's request for admin sign-up. Approval first, because an admin can approve pros, change prices and refund money. |
| D22 | 2026-11-05 | The email admins get about an account request contains a one-click approval link: opening it on the control panel approves the request, and the person can sign in with their email at once and is emailed that they are approved. The link works once, expires after 7 days, is stored only as a hash, and stops working once the request is answered on the Team page. No sign-in is needed to use it, because only approved admins receive it. | Owner's request: clicking the link in the sign-up email must let the new user sign in. |
| D23 | 2026-11-05 | Monthly plans: customers subscribe with a size (S −15%, L +35% of the medium price), as many weekdays as the frequency needs, a morning (08:00) or afternoon (13:00) window and the optional extra. One regular pro is chosen at sign-up (best-rated, free for every planned visit, covering the street, working those days) and visits are kept planned four weeks ahead. Billing is a monthly Stripe subscription; each paid month pays the pro their share. Skips are limited per calendar month; cancelling takes effect when the paid month ends, once the minimum term is over. Seasonal plans run April–October, cannot start in winter, and their billing pauses outside the season. One-off bookings treat planned visits as busy. | The plan page story: plans appear in the customer and pro apps and customers choose the extra when subscribing. |
| D24 | 2026-11-05 | Plan drafts save however incomplete; only publishing needs a name, category, price of at least €5 and one task. Leaving the plan page with unsaved changes asks first. The web app uses React Router's data router for this. | The plan page story. |
| D25 | 2026-11-06 | A finished job is charged 24 hours after completion, not at once. Until then the customer can report a problem in the app, which holds the charge until the team settles it: refund in full, or "Charge the customer". The settlement worker charges and pays the pro once the 24 hours pass with no open problem. Supersedes the "captured on completion" part of D5 and D15. | The admin bookings story: "the customer is charged 24 hours after the job is done if no problem is reported". Card holds last 7 days and bookings are at most 6 days ahead, so the extra day still fits inside the hold. |
| D26 | 2026-11-06 | The admin Bookings page lists one-off bookings and plan visits together. Each is in exactly one tab: Needs action (no pro, an open problem, still searching under 2 hours before the start, money still held on a cancelled job, a failed payout, or a plan visit whose time passed undone), Live now, Upcoming or Done. Plan visits of the last 90 days and the planning window are listed. Bookings and visits get readable numbers (B-1001, V-1001). Every team action on a booking (assign, offer again, move, cancel, refund, charge after a problem) is written to `booking_admin_actions` with who did it. Moving a booking keeps its length and is allowed until the pro sets off. Exports are built in the browser from exactly the rows shown, with formula-like cells neutralised. | The admin bookings story. |
| D27 | 2026-11-09 | Every pro passes four checks, one document each: ID card or passport, trade certificate, liability insurance, and a certificate of good conduct (Führungszeugnis) as the background check. The pro types the expiry date of the ID and insurance when uploading. A check is OK once the team accepts the document and it has not run out. Approve works only with all four OK. A new copy always waits for the team's check, but an earlier accepted copy keeps covering the pro until its own date, so renewing early never stops jobs. When cover runs out, a worker (every 15 minutes) moves the pro to Pending approval: no new offers, booked jobs stay. Accepting the new copy moves them back to Active. Pros approved before this change keep their documents as checked, with no expiry; their missing background check shows as Open with "Send reminder". | The Providers story. A background check is a document because Germany has no instant check service; the certificate of good conduct is what customers expect. |
| D28 | 2026-11-09 | Provider numbers cover the last 30 days. Acceptance: offers accepted ÷ offers answered or expired. On time: finished jobs where work started at most 10 minutes after the booked time. Regular customers: active monthly plans with the pro. Below 85% acceptance or 90% on time, the number is red; a quality warning also needs at least 5 offers or jobs (or 5 ratings for an average under 4.0), so one bad day does not flag a new pro. "Online right now": active, unpaused pros whose app was used in the last 15 minutes. "Documents expiring": accepted documents running out within 30 days. | The Providers story's thresholds; the sample size avoids false warnings. |
| D29 | 2026-11-09 | Pausing jobs keeps the pro Active but leaves them out of every new offer, slot and plan until resumed. Suspending needs a reason; open offers are withdrawn, booked one-off jobs that have not started go back to the search (the customer is texted), and each monthly plan gets a new regular pro with re-planned visits. Plans no free pro can take are reported to the admin and the customer is told a new pro is being found. Jobs already under way stay with the pro. Reinstating returns the pro to Active, or to Pending approval if a document ran out meanwhile. Every team action on a pro, and MISK's own move to Pending approval, is written to `pro_admin_actions`. | The Providers story ("give their open jobs to other pros"). Replaces Q11's earlier default. |
| D30 | 2026-11-09 | "Invite pro" copies `WEB_ORIGIN/signin?as=pro&method=phone`: phone sign-in, because job offers come by text, with "pro" already chosen on the role question. | The story asks for a link to copy; a personal invite token is not needed to sign up. |
| D31 | 2026-11-09 | A service is priced either by size (base price × Small 1.0 / Medium 1.3 / Large 1.7) or per hour. An hourly service has no sizes: a booking costs the hourly rate × the service's duration, fixed when booked, and the app shows "€38/h". The pro earns the Settings share of either. | The Catalogue story ("services paid per hour show /h"). Fixing the hours at booking keeps the card hold right and avoids a second charge. |
| D32 | 2026-11-09 | Catalogue: a new trade starts as a draft (not active) and customers cannot see or book it until it is published; it can go back to draft. Price changes apply to new bookings only, because a booking keeps the price it was made at. Every catalogue change (trade added, published, back to draft, edited; service added, edited, hidden, shown; price from → to) is logged with the admin in `catalogue_changes`. A service added in the last 14 days is marked "New" in the control panel and the app. The customer app reloads the catalogue every 30 seconds and when it comes back on screen, so changes show up straight away. "See in the app" draws the trade with the app's own screen inside the control panel (it works for drafts too), with a link to the live app for published trades. Trades show "pros" (all applicants and pros with the trade) and "verified pros" (approved). | The Catalogue story. |
| D33 | 2026-11-09 | Settings → Fees & payments: the service fee (€0–€100) and the MISK commission (10–30%, so pros get 70–90%) are copied onto each booking when it is made, so changes apply to new bookings only. Monthly plans keep their own share, set per plan. Pros are paid weekly (Fridays), every 2 weeks (every other Friday, counted from 2 January 2026) or monthly (the 1st). A worker sets each pro's Stripe payout schedule to match; Stripe has no two-weekly schedule, so for that MISK sets the schedule to manual and pays out the available balance itself on those Fridays (claimed in the database, with an idempotency key). The pro's profile shows the share, the next payout date and the bank account's last 4 digits. | The Settings story. |
| D34 | 2026-11-09 | Cancelling is free until 24 hours before the start. Later (pro booked, start under 24 hours away), the customer can still cancel but is first told the full price and must confirm; the hold is then charged, and the pro gets their share of the job price like a finished job (MISK keeps the rest and the fee). The team's cancellations stay free. Tips (€1–€200) are charged on the card saved at booking once the job is done, split with the commission of the moment, and paid out with the job (straight away if the job is already charged). A tip that would need the customer to confirm with their bank is not taken. Refunding a job leaves the tip with the pro. | The Settings story; replaces "tips not in Phase 1" (Q6). |
| D35 | 2026-11-09 | Team roles: Admin (everything), Operations (overview, bookings, providers, plans, catalogue, service area, notifications), Support (overview, bookings, providers), Finance (overview, bookings, fees & payments). The API checks the role on every /admin request by its address, and the control panel hides what a role cannot open. There is always at least one Admin. "Invite teammate" creates the account with the chosen role and emails a link (valid 7 days, works once) to choose a password; admins created any other way get the Admin role. | The Settings story's roles; the access per role is MISK's default until the owner says otherwise (Q19). |
| D36 | 2026-11-09 | Messages go out on push, SMS and email from one outbox, one row per channel. The eight messages on the Settings page (booking confirmed, reminder the evening before at 18:00, pro on the way, extra work needs approval, job done with photos, ask for a rating 2 hours after the job, new job offer, payout sent) have their channels chosen there; other messages go by SMS, or email for people without a phone. Push is Web Push to the app (VAPID keys in `.env`); people turn it on per device under Account. A browser that no longer accepts push is forgotten. "Send me a test" sends the chosen message to the admin's own phone (set on the Notifications tab, not used for sign-in), email and devices. | The Settings story. Native app push waits for the Phase 3 apps (D4). |
| D37 | 2026-11-09 | Service area: each served postcode is a zone, live (customers can book) or on the waitlist. Customers whose postcode is not served, or paused, join its waitlist from the booking page. "Go live" opens a zone and messages everyone waiting; "Pause" closes it for new customers, while booked jobs go ahead. The map is drawn from the zones' centre points, without an outside map service. Settings saves every change by itself and shows "All changes saved"; each change, with who made it and when, goes into `admin_activity`, and the Team tab shows it together with the booking, provider and catalogue logs, newest first. | The Settings story. |
| D38 | 2026-11-11 | Overview: the first page after signing in, for the city in `MARKET_CITY` (or the time zone's city). Today runs from midnight in the market against yesterday until the same time; 7 and 30 days against the 7 or 30 days before. Bookings are one-off bookings made in the period; the chart counts one-off jobs and plan visits together in the week they take place, over 4, 8 or 13 weeks for Today, 7 or 30 days, with this week highlighted. On time uses the 10-minute grace of D28. Pro applications (oldest first, five shown) are only sent to roles that may open Providers; ready ones can be approved on the spot. Live bookings are the eight starting closest to now, refreshed every 15 seconds. | The Overview story; the design was not attached, so the chart's weeks per period are our choice. |
| D39 | 2026-11-11 | Email sign-up: the account is created but cannot sign in until the email is confirmed through a single-use link valid for 24 hours (only a hash is stored; a new link replaces the old one; at most 5 resends per email and 20 per network address within the attempt window). Opening the link signs the person in. Log-in says "confirm your email first" only after the right password, so it does not reveal which emails have accounts. Accounts made before this count as confirmed. The terms and conditions are in the app (`/terms`, and a panel in the sign-up form) and describe how MISK works; `VITE_TERMS_URL` still takes over on the log-in page once the reviewed terms are published. | The sign-up story: read the terms, verify the email before logging in. |
| D40 | 2026-11-11 | Plans can be deleted from the plan editor after a confirmation, drafts and live plans alike, as long as no customer has ever subscribed (any status, including cancelled): subscriptions keep a link to their plan. Such plans can only be unpublished, which stops new sign-ups while existing plans keep running. Deleting is permanent; there is no archive. | The owner asked for deleting plans. |
| D41 | 2026-11-11 | Customers can add up to 5 photos (JPEG or PNG, 8 MB each) when they report a problem, and more while it is open. The photos are kept as `problem` booking photos and shown to the MISK team in the booking panel under the customer's note, also after the problem is settled. The pro does not see them, in the job view or through the file link. Other booking photos (before and after the job) are listed separately. Booking lists show the 10 most recent bookings with "View all": the customer's list in its own order (open first, newest first), the admin list in its order (needs action first, then closest to now). | The owner asked for problem photos for the admin, and shorter booking lists. |
| D42 | 2026-11-11 | Forms never grey out their main button because something is missing or wrong. Clicking it shows, under each field, what to fix (e.g. "Enter your email address", "The code has 6 digits"), marks the field red (`aria-invalid`, described by the message), moves the cursor to the first one and sends nothing; messages then update as the person types. The checks match the API (email and phone format, password rules, lengths). When the API still refuses input it answers `validation_failed` with the `field`, and the app says which one to check. A test fails if the API gains an error code without a message in the app. Buttons that wait for something outside the form (a photo before "Job done", a time slot, the off-season) keep saying what they wait for. | The owner asked for clear error messages. |
| D43 | 2026-11-11 | Every message to a customer or pro is also kept in their Messages in the app (`inbox`), with an unread count on the tab, so a job assigned by the team or a cancelled job reaches the pro even without text messages or push; opening Messages marks them read. "Booking cancelled" now also goes out by push. The booking and plan visit panels show a customer profile: contact, how they sign in, customer since, bookings (done, coming up, cancelled), money paid and tips, active plans, ratings given, problems reported and late cancellations. The CSV export starts with the tab, search and filters it was taken with and names them in the file name. "Offer it to pros again" is gone from the panel: the team assigns a pro directly (the API can still restart a search). | The Bookings story. |
| D44 | 2026-11-12 | A trade can be removed from the Catalogue ("Remove trade", after a confirmation) together with its services, but only while nothing depends on it: bookings or monthly subscriptions in it, plans for it, or pros offering it each keep it, and the admin is told which and to unpublish it instead. Its own change log goes with it, so the removal is written to the team's activity log (area "catalogue"). The trade's activity log on the Catalogue page is folded by default and opens with a click on its title, showing how many changes it holds. | The Catalogue story; the owner chose removing a whole trade. |
| D45 | 2026-11-12 | In a pro's panel, "Send reminder" darkens while pressed and turns into a green "Reminder sent at 10:22" once sent. It stays so for the hour in which the API refuses another reminder about the same document (it reads the reminder from the pro's log, so a reload keeps it), then the button comes back with "Last reminder: …". Payouts (bank and next payout), recent payouts, reviews and the activity log are folded sections that open with a click on their title and show what they hold (amount or count). | The Providers story. |
| D46 | 2026-11-12 | Each service row in the Catalogue has a Remove button (after a confirmation). A service that was never booked is deleted, and its earlier change-log lines keep its name. A service with bookings is kept in the database, because bookings point at it, but marked removed and hidden: it leaves the Catalogue list, the counts and the customer app, and can no longer be edited or shown again. | D47 | 2026-11-12 | Settings → Service area checks a new or edited zone's centre point before saving it. Latitude and longitude must each be a plain decimal number (dot or comma) within -90…90 and -180…180, must not be the same number (the reported mistake: one value copied into both fields, which also covers 0, 0), and must lie within 100 km of the nearest other zone, because MISK serves one city and a point further away is almost always latitude and longitude swapped. The first zone has nothing to compare with and is accepted anywhere. The form names the problem under the field and sends nothing; the API refuses the same cases (`coordinates_identical`, `zone_too_far`) so no other client can save them. Coordinates copied from a map as one text ("52.5200, 13.4050") and pasted into either field fill both. |
| D48 | 2026-11-12 | Every change to a teammate's account is saved in the activity log with who did it, what and when, like the other Settings changes: creating an admin account, setting a new password, changing the email, and approving or declining an account request. | D49 | 2026-11-12 | The Settings page follows the product demo's Settings screen (misk-demo 4.1): title with "The rules the marketplace runs on · changes save automatically" and a green "All changes saved" badge; a side menu with icons for Fees & payments, Service area, Notifications and Team & access (a scrolling row on narrow screens); each section is one card of rows with the setting on the left and its control on the right. | D50 | 2026-11-12 | Customers and pros who sign in with email can reset a forgotten password, like admins already could. "Forgot password?" under the password on the log-in page opens /forgot-password, which checks the email format and always answers "If this email belongs to a MISK account, a reset link is on its way", so it cannot reveal who has an account. The emailed link (/reset-password#token=…, the token never reaches server logs) works once and for 30 minutes; asking again replaces the older link; at most 3 links per email and 10 per network address in 15 minutes. | D51 | 2026-11-12 | Team & access: teammates can no longer change each other's email or password from the control panel; the buttons are gone and the API routes for it (POST /admin/team/:id/password, PATCH /admin/team/:id) are removed.
